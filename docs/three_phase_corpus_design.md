# Three-Phase Corpus Design

## Revised Corpus Logic

The project now uses three large evidence blocks rather than one continuous small seed:

1. **1946-1987: Taiwan-side formation corpus**
   - Minimum: 120 verified/probable attestations.
   - Focus: postwar food hierarchy, lu rou fan as stall/market food, Taiwan-side restaurantization, brand formalization, service marks, branch formation.

2. **1987-2015: cross-strait diffusion and capital/commercial gateway corpus**
   - Minimum: 120+ verified/probable attestations.
   - Focus: cross-strait mobility, Taiwan-capital and Taiwan-style chains, Shanghai/YRD gateway, Expo/event visibility, early platform/merchant listings.

3. **2015-2025: internet / wanghong / capital-event corpus**
   - Minimum: separate capital/platform event corpus, not directly comparable to earlier semantic corpora.
   - Focus: investment, registered capital, operating entities, mall partnerships, store-count acceleration, franchise portals, platform rankings, prepared-food/supply-chain controversies.

## Why 2015-2025 Is Separate

After 2015, textual visibility is heavily shaped by search engines, Dianping/Xiaohongshu-style platform ranking, SEO franchise portals, influencer food media, short-video circulation, and shopping-mall branding. A simple count of "Taiwanese lu rou fan" terms would mostly measure internet-platform amplification, not organic semantic diffusion.

Therefore, for 2015-2025, the main analytical unit should be:

```text
capital/platform event
```

rather than only:

```text
semantic attestation
```

## Phase-Specific Evidence Units

### 1946-1987

Valid units:

- newspaper mention;
- menu or recipe;
- market/stall record;
- image or photo archive;
- brand timeline event;
- trademark/service-mark record;
- oral history or cultural memory record.

Primary questions:

- When does lu rou fan become visible as a Taiwan-side popular food?
- Which urban geographies matter: Taipei, Shuanglian, Ningxia, Yuanhuan, Huaxi Street, Tainan/southern variants?
- When does stall food become shop/brand/formalized food?

### 1987-2015

Valid units:

- cross-strait business report;
- Taiwan-capital restaurant record;
- mainland store-opening report;
- business registration;
- event/public-expo source;
- restaurant listing;
- menu/platform listing;
- consumer guide or food-media record.

Primary questions:

- Which Taiwan food categories entered mainland first?
- Was lu rou fan central, or did it follow broader Taiwan-food and Taiwan-capital channels?
- Was Shanghai/YRD the primary gateway?
- How did Taiwan-capital/JV/chain formats differ from later mainland Taiwan-themed formats?

### 2015-2025

Valid units:

- financing event;
- registered capital / shareholder record;
- new operating company;
- store-count report;
- mall partnership;
- franchise portal;
- prepared-food/supply-chain controversy;
- platform ranking event;
- brand hiring page with company type/capital;
- commercial-real-estate brand listing.

Primary questions:

- Is growth driven by Taiwan capital, mainland operators, Hong Kong intermediary brands, or mall/platform systems?
- Does Taiwaneseness become a detachable format in franchise/SEO/wanghong economy?
- Does ownership or operating structure shift faster than menu semantics?

## Stop Rule

No phase-level inference until:

- 1946-1987 has at least 120 verified/probable attestations;
- 1987-2015 has at least 120 verified/probable attestations;
- 2015-2025 has enough capital/platform events to classify at least 30 brands or operating cases.
