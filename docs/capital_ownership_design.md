# Capital and Ownership Layer

## Why This Layer Is Necessary

The project cannot assume that "Taiwanese lu rou fan" in mainland China is driven by Taiwan capital or Taiwanese migrants. A restaurant can perform Taiwaneseness while being:

- Taiwan-owned;
- Taiwan-mainland joint venture;
- Hong Kong-founded with Taiwanese chef/origin narrative;
- mainland-founded Taiwan-themed brand;
- franchise/operator-driven platform format;
- mall/commercial-real-estate-driven wanghong economy.

This distinction is central to the argument. If recent "Taiwanese lu rou fan" growth is mostly mainland/Hong Kong/platform capital using Taiwaneseness as a brandable sign, then the paper is no longer only about cultural diffusion from Taiwan. It becomes a study of how Taiwaneseness is re-authored through mainland capital, urban commercial real estate, and platform visibility.

## Core Question

> Is the mainland diffusion of Taiwanese lu rou fan driven by Taiwan capital, Taiwan-origin culinary migration, Hong Kong intermediary branding, mainland local operators, or platform/mall wanghong economics?

## Ownership Categories

### taiwan_capital

The operating or parent company is clearly Taiwan-based or Taiwan-funded.

Evidence:

- registered Taiwan company;
- Taiwan parent company;
- Taiwan investor or founder with controlling role;
- official Taiwan business directory source.

### taiwan_mainland_joint_venture

The brand or mainland operating company is jointly structured between Taiwan and mainland capital.

Evidence:

- documented joint venture;
- shareholder split;
- Taiwan business + mainland corporate partner.

### hong_kong_intermediary

The brand is founded or primarily developed in Hong Kong while using Taiwanese culinary expertise, Taiwan-origin story, or Taiwanese chef/founder narrative.

Evidence:

- official brand page says Hong Kong founder plus Taiwanese chef;
- Hong Kong store origin before mainland expansion.

### mainland_taiwan_themed

The brand is mainland-origin but uses Taiwaneseness as theme, cuisine category, design, menu language, or platform identity.

Evidence:

- source describes brand as founded in Shanghai or another mainland city;
- no clear Taiwan capital/founder evidence;
- Taiwaneseness appears mainly as commercial style or cuisine label.

### franchise_or_operator_capital

The brand is shaped by franchise operators, local partners, or store-level investors rather than a single origin capital.

Evidence:

- franchise documents;
- local operating companies;
- multiple regional operators.

### mall_platform_wanghong

Growth is strongly tied to shopping malls, commercial real estate groups, online queueing, viral media, Michelin/award signaling, consumer-downgrade discourse, or platform visibility.

Evidence:

- source emphasizes mall partnerships;
- high turnover, queueing, viral expansion;
- commercial real estate partners;
- price/value discourse;
- platform review or Xiaohongshu/Dianping circulation.

## Required Fields

Each brand or case should record:

- brand
- operating_company
- founding_year
- founding_place
- founder_origin
- ownership_category
- capital_origin
- mainland_entry_year
- mainland_entry_city
- source_url

- confidence
- notes

## Analytical Use

Capital origin should be crossed with period and geography:

```text
period x corridor x ownership_category x Taiwaneseness domain score
```

This allows hypotheses such as:

1. Taiwan-capital or Taiwan-mainland JV brands in the 2000s frame Taiwaneseness as broad casual dining and urban leisure.
2. Hong Kong intermediary brands frame Taiwaneseness through authenticity, night-market food, Michelin/award authority, and chef-origin narrative.
3. Mainland Taiwan-themed brands frame Taiwaneseness as platform-friendly small-store food, value pricing, and everyday lunch.
4. Mall/platform wanghong expansion weakens Taiwan as origin and strengthens Taiwan as scalable commercial format.

## Interpretation Rule

Never infer capital origin from menu language. "Taiwanese" in the shop name is not evidence of Taiwan ownership. Ownership claims require business-registration data, official brand sources, credible media reports, or investor/franchise records.
